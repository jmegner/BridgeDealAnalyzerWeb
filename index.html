    FROM DEAL TO DISCOVERY

    A study companion for your bridge hands.

    Import one or more PBN files containing complete 52-card deals. DDS3 calculates the double-dummy table and dealer par: the score and contracts reached by perfect bidding with all cards known, including sacrifices and vulnerability.

    For each board, we choose one par contract and generate a deterministic line of optimal play. Every chosen card preserves the double-dummy result.

    What a technique label means

    A label identifies a specific pattern demonstrated in that replay. It does not mean the technique was necessary, or that other lines have been searched. “No technique identified” means these detectors did not find a supported pattern in this line.

    The first version recognizes simple cashing squeezes, elimination with a throw-in, direct trump-coup endings, dummy reversals, and repeated crossruffs. Complex squeezes and other variants can be missed. Each finding includes the evidence used to identify it.

    Your files stay with you

    Files are read and analyzed on your device. Results are saved in this browser automatically. Export results to keep a separate JSON copy. Clearing your browser’s site data removes the saved collection.

    Large batches can take time. Stop keeps completed results; Analyze resumes unfinished hands. One difficult hand can be skipped.
